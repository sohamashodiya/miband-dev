# Vela calibration kit: project notes

Read this before working on the kit. It only applies to the kit (the facts it measures live in the
workspace's `devices/<model>/profile.json`, or the plugin's shipped default, and apply to every band app on that model).

## What and who
- A developer tool, not a product: a band app of full-screen test patterns, `measure.py` that reads
  photos of them, and the device profiles every band app sizes its UI from. The user takes the
  photos; nobody else uses the app. See `README.md` for the pages, the photo procedure and the schema.
- No style rules beyond measurability: black ground, white ink, saturated 1 px outlines (pink,
  cyan, green) that are never white, yellow only for the ruler 100s and the scale bar.

## Devices
- Band 10 Pro (336 × 480, fw 3.101.043): the first profile (shipped as `data/profiles/band10pro/profile.json`;
  a workspace copy in `devices/band10pro/` overrides it). Measured on the band that ran the BART Watch app.
- Band 10 / 11 (212 × 520 capsule, fw 4.100.139): kit 2.0.0 builds `dist/com.soham.velacalib.band11.debug.2.0.0.rpk`;
  measured 2026-09-28/29 (below; `CALIBRATION.md` next to the profile).

## Code
- Package `com.soham.velacalib` (rename it in `gen/gen.mjs` if you like; nothing measured depends on it),
  signed with the workspace's shared key (`sign/`, written by the new-project skill's `make-keys.sh`). No
  phone companion. `npm run build` → `dist/com.soham.velacalib.<device>.debug.2.0.0.rpk` (one per model); `npm test`.
- Everything under `src/` is generated by `gen/` (don't hand-edit `index.ux`).

## Design project
- None. The pages are test patterns whose content is fixed by what they measure. If the user wants
  mock-ups anyway, add one board per page from `layouts/<model>.json`.

## Kit 2.0.0 (2026-09-28): the 1.0.0 layout fixes, and the Band 10 / 11
Built, tested (`npm test`: profile + layout tests for both models, `measure.py selftest` PASS for
both), not yet installed on any band.
- **One .rpk per model** (`gen/build.mjs`): `designWidth` = the screen width (336 / 212), so the
  rulers are band px. Chosen over one rpk choosing its layout at runtime: that needs one designWidth
  for both screens (a string `designWidth` like "device-width" is allowed by the manifest schema but
  unverified on Vela), and doubling the pages in one index.ux. Same package, so one replaces the other.
- **Self-checking scale:** start + geometry pages print `@system.device` getInfo (screen size,
  shape, density, window/logic size when present). On a capsule `measure.py` fits the end arcs and
  writes `device.design_scale` (W/2 over the end radius; 1:1 when within 6 % and the arc centre is
  within 4 px of W/2) and only then `device.design_width`; it prints a warning otherwise. A page
  drawn larger than the panel loses its right ruler, and the fit says so.
- **Text V:** boxes are the string's worst-case width (fit.js table raised to the Band 10 Pro's
  measured advances, x1.2 plus 1 px per glyph for a new model; x1.08 on the Band 10 Pro), natural
  slots 1.5 em tall (1.4 on the Band 10 Pro), so nothing wraps and fixed boxes measure the plain
  regime. Two deliberate "8H" boxes (cut through the H) measure the too-wide regime (`too_wide` in
  the layout; measure.py matches the glyphs that survive the cut).
- **Text W:** strings that can't fit the screen width are made smaller (to 20 px), halved (repeated
  digits) or split at a space; BART's strings only on the Band 10 Pro.
- **Wrap:** slot heights from a worst-case greedy word wrap at 1.5 em per line; the line-height
  boxes are 136 wide so "Hg8" at 48 can't wrap.
- **Box:** 80 x 96 slots (every hypothesis inside); overflow tests use an absolute child; new
  `css.flex_shrink_zero` test (white shrink 0 + cyan shrink 1 in a row too narrow for both).
- **Images:** fit-cover on `wide64x32_q.png` (white block at source columns 16-31); images pack
  over as many pages as needed (2 on the Band 11); the alpha ramp is 128 px wide there.
- **Animation:** 8 s runs, finish marker (yellow from 90 % of the path: keyframes with
  background-color, which the compiler keeps; lane F, an image, dims instead), a yellow tick under
  each lane at 90 %, and lane H = BART's pattern (class `{{hv.c}}`, `this.hv` replaced every 1 s).
  Lane A must turn yellow, or the marker itself doesn't work on that band.
- **Frame kept clear:** every sample has a worst-case `slot`; `test/layout.test.mjs` checks (both
  models) slots on screen, inside the visible outline (capsule ends / 48 px corners, 4 px margin),
  clear of rulers, bar, barcode and label, no overlaps, <= 32 pages.
- **measure.py:** `cap` samples (capsule end: radius, centre, hidden end rows, hidden columns from
  the straight rows, bloom-corrected to the safe side), `screen.shape` / `end_radius_px` /
  `corner_radius_px` per corner, hidden-column estimates from ruler ticks on straight rows only
  (`frame.straight_y`), evidence keyed by (layout, kit version, page) so 1.0.0 and 2.0.0 photos
  both count, `selftest [--device X]` renders both models (capsule mask, 2 px hidden per side).
  Checked: re-measuring the 17 real 1.0.0 photos with `layouts/legacy-kit-1.0.0-band10pro.json`
  rebuilds the Band 10 Pro profile identically.
- Band 10 Pro: 21 pages (was 19); re-shoot all of 1-19 when convenient (not needed for BART).
- **Band 10 / 11 notes:** 24 pages (22 photos + start-page photo + watched animation page). Area
  x 24-188, y 52-456 (everything >= 4 px inside a 106 px-radius capsule); bar 160 x 4 at (26, 462),
  barcode 62 px wide at (75, 474) under it; label centred at y 26; ruler ticks visible from about
  y 75 to 445 (~37 per side, 6 yellow). Text V to 140 px only.
- Unsure until the band shows it: that designWidth 212 renders 1:1 on the Band 11 (BandLink uses
  designWidth 336 plus a pill-shaped media query and was never measured); keyframe background-color
  (lane A tells); `windowLogicWidth` in getInfo; whether the system draws anything over the capsule ends.

## First Band 11 run (2026-09-28/29, a Band 11, fw 4.100.139)
- 23 photos (pages 0-22, Pixel 10 main camera in macro-focus mode, 4000 x 3000, band lying
  sideways) + retakes of pages 11, 14, 19, 21 in `devices/band11/photos/retake1/`. **All 27 fit**
  (ruler rms 0.21-0.37 px, 58-76 ticks, 3.5-4.9 photo px per band px) after the measure.py fixes
  below; every sample measures. The retakes win for their pages (measured last); the main-set
  shots of those pages would also have done. No page needs re-shooting.
- **Why 4 pages failed first (measurer bugs, not the photos):**
  - the scale bar's direction was taken as "pointing right in the photo" (`u[0] >= 0`). With the
    band lying sideways the bar is near-vertical, so a slight tilt flipped the page 180 degrees:
    0 yellow ticks matched and the barcode was read upside down (page 11 "barcode unreadable",
    14, 21). Fit now tries both ends of the bar and keeps the one the yellow ticks agree with.
  - yellow ticks were matched within 30 x 25 px of where the bar predicts them, with no shape
    check: the geometry page's yellow `{{info}}` line (and other yellow near the rulers) became
    "ticks", and the fit locked 3-7 px off (page 19 rms 1.2-2.3, others 1.4-2.0). A yellow blob now
    counts only if it is tick-shaped (0.5-1.6 x 22 px long, <= 4 px thick) within 14 x 15 px; the
    refinement passes use RANSAC homographies.
- **Other measure.py fixes found on these photos** (Band 10 Pro 1.0.0 photos re-measured: every
  verdict the same, numbers within 0.1 px):
  - the Band 11's white photographs lavender (about 95, 130, 215; min/max 0.44, the old 'neutral'
    score expected ~0.6): white is now judged per photo against the ruler ticks' own colour
    (`ruler_white`, `Rect(rect, white=...)`, chromaticity over ~1 band px); colour_at names use it;
    cyan as this camera sees it (13, 128, 223) is named cyan.
  - `mask_bbox` takes its peak and mask over a ~1 band px blur (the panel's pixel grid made a
    64 x 32 pill read as 58 x 8); ring and corner tests (white alone in their region) use luma.
    Pill: square -> clamped; outline radius: ignored -> honoured; override: honoured -> ignored
    (the photo agrees each time).
  - corner check under a box's bottom edge: 3-6 px below, < 0.5 of the side (bloom read 0.40 at
    2-5 px and rejected three boxes at the bottom of their pages).
  - a regime's `clipped` is judged on the digit, not all ink (a 24 px "g" 1.3 px from the bottom of
    a 1.5 em box had flagged taller boxes as clipped).
  - colour floor: black is the page's empty background (now measured; the 8 px-wide grey
    swatches catch bloom, #000000 read luma 45 over a black of 3), red / green / blue ramps judged
    on their own channel. A grey row whose #000000 reads above black is marked `contaminated`.
- **Band 11 results:** design scale 1.025 (1:1, designWidth 212 correct), end radius 103.3 / 103.4
  px, arc centre within 0.3 px of x 106, hidden top 1.8 / bottom 0.25 / left 0.9 / right 1.9 px
  (the two page-19 photos agree within ~1.5 px on the ends). Line box 1.331 em, same ink as the
  Band 10 Pro (digit 0.253-1.064 em), taller boxes centre, shorter boxes top-anchor and clip,
  too-wide strings top-anchor. z-index ignored, per-corner radius ignored, flex-shrink 0 honoured.
- **Animation (watched 2026-09-28, recorded with `measure.py set`):** keyframe `background-color`
  does NOT animate on the Band 11, so the finish marker never shows (lane A never turns yellow;
  `finish_marker` false; use opacity or transform for markers); B restarts every 3 s (a class
  swap restarts); H restarts every 1 s (a class bound to a field of an object replaced every
  second restarts, BART's bug); C (unchanged binding, 1 s re-render) and F (image src swap) don't
  restart; E runs off the screen (600 px translate works); D steps; G fades.
- Next kit version (only if pages change anyway): draw the geometry page's device line in grey
  (yellow is reserved for the rulers and bar), wider grey swatches (8 px catch bloom), and an
  opacity-based finish marker.
