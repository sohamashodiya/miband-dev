# Vela calibration kit: project notes

Read this before working on the kit. It only applies to the kit (the facts it measures live in
`devices/<model>/profile.json` at the repo root and apply to every band app on that model).

## What and who
- A developer tool, not a product: a band app of full-screen test patterns, `measure.py` that reads
  photos of them, and the device profiles every band app sizes its UI from. The user takes the
  photos; nobody else uses the app. See `README.md` for the pages, the photo procedure and the schema.
- No style rules beyond measurability: black ground, white ink, saturated 1 px outlines (pink,
  cyan, green) that are never white, yellow only for the ruler 100s and the scale bar.

## Devices
- Band 10 Pro (336 × 480, fw 3.101.043): the first profile, `devices/band10pro/profile.json`. Measured on a
  Band 10 Pro paired to an Android phone (see `devices/DEVICES.md`).
- Band 10 / 11 (212 × 520): `node gen/gen.mjs band11` generates a layout; not built or photographed yet (`devices/band11/CALIBRATION.md`).

## Code
- Package `com.soham.velacalib`, shared BandLink signing key (`sign/` = copy of `apps/bandlink/band/sign`). No
  phone companion. `npm run build` → `dist/com.soham.velacalib.debug.1.0.0.rpk`; `npm test`.
- Everything under `src/` is generated by `gen/` (don't hand-edit `index.ux`).

## Design project
- None. The pages are test patterns whose content is fixed by what they measure. If the user wants
  the kit on the canvas anyway, add one board per page from `layouts/band10pro.json`.

## State (2026-09-27)
- 1.0.0 installed and photographed 2026-09-27 (below). Profile first seeded from the two BART calibration photos (`calib.jpg` =
  2.0.11 screen, `calib2.jpg` = 2.0.12 check screen) re-measured with `measure.py` via
  `layouts/legacy-bart-2.0.1{1,2}.json` (evidence now in `devices/band10pro/evidence/`), plus facts
  carried over from BART's notes, each marked measured or assumed.
- Key finding re-measured from calib2.jpg: a `<text>` **shorter** than its line box is NOT centred:
  the line stays top-aligned (ink where it is in a natural box) and the ink is cut at the box bottom.
  Taller boxes centre. So BART 2.0.12's tight boxes (e.g. a 240 px digit in 224) clip.
- Ruler fit on those photos: 85–86 ticks, rms 0.24–0.30 px (x) / 0.13 px (y); bloom 1.3 px.

## First full run (2026-09-27, Band 10 Pro, 17 photos IMG20260927202514-202836, handheld)
- All 17 photos fit (ruler rms 0.23-0.43 px). measure.py fixes needed on real photos (all in 1.0.0's
  measure.py now; selftest still passes): blur the luma before tick detection (a sharp close-up
  resolves the panel's pixel grid, 1.39 px rms on page 1 before); bar ends that step over a line
  drawn across the bar; outline edges by median then 85th percentile (ink drawn over a box's edge);
  explicit-size boxes infer a hidden side from the other; ink masks drop specks and outline glow;
  wrapped natural boxes are measured per line, and samples a wrapped box grows over are rejected;
  wrap content height was 2 px short; ellipsis dots with camera bloom; a 'neutral' white score and
  largest-component bboxes for the box pages; exact hypothesis ties are recorded as "a|b"; fact
  keys with dots (':.,-/%', 'image.x0.5') no longer split into nested objects; the screen corner
  radius is a circle fit to the corner square's visible edge (1.0.0 assumed the arc ended before the
  square's middle row and read 24-28 px; the fit gives 46-48.5 px, rms 0.12-0.19 px).
- Page 2's barcode was covered by overflowing text: measured with `--page 2`.
- Layout bugs in 1.0.0 (fix in the next kit version, then re-shoot pages 1-4, 10, 11): the Text V
  "Hg8" boxes are 0.93x the string's width, so every natural one wraps and grows over its
  neighbours (tv4.2 over tv4.3/tv4.5, tv2.2 off the page), and the fixed ones measure the
  "too wide" regime instead of the plain one; the Wrap page's slots are too short for 4 lines, so
  left/center/right and height40 overlap (read by eye); Box 2 overflow tests are confounded by
  flex-shrink (use flex-shrink 0 or an absolute child); fit-cover needs a patterned image.
- Animation page: no finish marker, so "smooth" can't rule out a restart at 3 s (lanes B, F).
  Next version: keyframes that turn the square yellow for the last 10 % (it only turns yellow if it
  gets there), an 8 s cycle, and a lane that reproduces BART's pattern (class reading an object
  that is replaced every second).


## Repo reorganisation (2026-09-28)
- Profiles moved to `devices/<model>/profile.json`, evidence to `devices/<model>/evidence/`, photos to
  `devices/<model>/photos/` (gitignored). `measure.py` takes `--device <model>` (default band10pro) or
  `--profile PATH`; `profile-node.mjs` has `profilePath()` and `DEVICES_DIR`.
- Profile schema 2: top-level `firmware`, `device`, `screen`; `quickapp` and `face` sections. `profile.js`
  resolves short quick-app paths inside `quickapp` (so the API is unchanged) and has `firmware()`.
  `export.mjs` drops `face` and has `--check` (the BART fit test uses it). Checked: a full
  `measure.py run` from all 19 evidence files on a copy reproduced the profile exactly; BART's old and
  new `device.js` agree on 92,831 helper calls.
